Not every image generator is good at making artwork you can trace. Vector art needs flat colors, clean edges, a single subject and a plain background; many models, left to themselves, add texture, scenery and shading that turn into thousands of tiny shapes. We ran the same prompts through the five models in VTracer's [Gen AI lab](/kb/gen-ai-lab) and traced every result with VTracer 2 to find out which one makes the best vectors.

## Same prompts, five models

Each prompt described a style and ended with the same instructions: *of a girl with short hair, pure white background, centered, simple composition*. Every model ran at 512 × 512 with its default number of steps, mostly with the same seed.

## What the comparison shows

- **The 2023 models ignore half the prompt.** SD-Turbo and SDXL-Turbo picked up the style words but not *pure white background* or *simple composition*: they painted scenes, borders, extra characters and garbled lettering. Every brushstroke of that becomes shapes, so their traces are several times heavier.
- **A language-model text encoder makes the difference.** The three newer models read prompts with a large language model, and they did what was asked: one figure, centered, on white.
- **Z-Image Turbo is the best for prompt to vector.** It handles every art style in the lab and a wide range of illustration styles, and its results were the flattest and simplest, typically 70 paths and under 30 KB, close to what an illustrator would draw by hand. Occasionally it is too restrained: one anime portrait came out pale and washed out. It is less strong when it starts from an image: it sticks closely to a sketch, which leaves it less freedom to draw.
- **FLUX.2 klein is the best for image-to-image.** In Pencil to vector it keeps the pose and expression of a sketch while still drawing it its own way, and it is the best at changing an existing picture with a reference image. It is also the fastest of the modern models in our test, but from a prompt alone its results are not as good as Z-Image Turbo's.
- **FLUX.1 schnell draws the finest line work,** with more detail and shading than the other two modern models, at the cost of larger files, the biggest download and the slowest rounds.

## Which to choose

| If you want | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| Artwork from a prompt, with every art style and the cleanest, smallest SVGs: start here | [Z-Image Turbo](/kb/z-image-vector-art) |
| Artwork from your sketch, or an existing image edited with a reference | [FLUX.2 klein](/kb/flux-vector-art) |
| Detailed illustration and fine line work | [FLUX.1 schnell](/kb/flux-vector-art) |
| Rich, painterly detail, accepting heavier files | [SDXL-Turbo](/kb/stable-diffusion-vector-art) |
| The fastest rounds on modest hardware | [SD-Turbo](/kb/stable-diffusion-vector-art) |
